CommunityLab is an AI-powered community intelligence platform. It takes raw Discord message exports, runs them through a LangGraph processing pipeline to detect achievements, recurring questions, and blockers, then automatically drafts content assets ready for your team to review and publish.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ingest a message batch">
    Upload a JSON, JSONL, or CSV file of Discord messages via the web UI or the REST API. CommunityLab validates, normalizes, and deduplicates every record before storing it in PostgreSQL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI pipeline processes the batch">
    The async worker picks up the job and runs it through the LangGraph graph: classification, relevance scoring, and routing to one of four content paths — achievement post, FAQ, community highlights, or internal alert.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and approve assets">
    Generated drafts land in the Curation panel. Curators edit copy, transition assets through the state machine (generated → reviewed → approved), and add review comments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and store">
    Approved assets are persisted to OCI Object Storage under a structured path by community origin and period, ready for downstream publishing workflows.
  </Step>
</Steps>
